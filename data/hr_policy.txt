Company HR Policy Handbook

1. Work Hours and Attendance

Standard working hours are from 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM, Monday through Friday.

Employees are expected to be available during core collaboration hours between 10:00 AM and 3:00 PM.

Hybrid employees must attend office locations at least two days per week unless otherwise approved by management.

Repeated late attendance without prior approval may result in HR review.

2. Leave Policy

Full-time employees are eligible for:

- 10 paid sick leaves annually
- 15 vacation days annually
- 3 personal emergency leave days
- Maternity and paternity leave according to provincial regulations

Vacation requests must be submitted at least 14 days in advance through the HR portal.

Unused vacation days may be carried forward up to a maximum of 5 days.

3. Payroll and Compensation

Payroll is processed biweekly.

Employees must report payroll discrepancies within 5 business days.

Payroll inquiries should be directed to:

payroll@company.com

Direct deposit information can be updated through the employee self-service portal.

4. Code of Conduct

Employees are expected to maintain professional behavior and respect workplace diversity and inclusion principles.

Harassment, discrimination, or workplace misconduct will not be tolerated.

Violations may result in disciplinary action or termination.

5. Cybersecurity and Compliance

All employees must complete mandatory cybersecurity awareness training within the first 7 days of joining.

Employees must use multi-factor authentication for all company systems.

Sharing passwords or confidential company data is strictly prohibited.

Any suspected phishing attempts must be reported immediately to:

security@company.com

6. Remote Work Policy

Remote employees must maintain secure internet connections and comply with VPN usage requirements.

Sensitive company data must not be stored on personal devices.

Company-issued devices must be used for all official work.

7. Performance Reviews

Performance evaluations are conducted twice annually.

Employees are evaluated on:

- Technical performance
- Collaboration
- Communication
- Innovation
- Leadership contribution

Managers provide quarterly feedback sessions.
