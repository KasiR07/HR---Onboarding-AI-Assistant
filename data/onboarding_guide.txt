New Employee Onboarding Guide

Welcome to the company.

This onboarding guide is designed to help new employees transition smoothly into their role and understand company processes, tools, and expectations.

1. Day 1 Activities

On the first day, employees are required to:

- Attend HR orientation
- Collect company-issued laptop and accessories
- Activate company email account
- Configure multi-factor authentication
- Join the company Slack workspace
- Complete workstation setup
- Review onboarding checklist

Employees will receive onboarding documentation and department-specific training schedules.

2. IT Setup Process

The IT department provides support for:

- Laptop provisioning
- VPN access
- Software installation
- Email setup
- Account permissions
- MFA configuration

New hires must verify all applications are functioning correctly before the end of the first week.

For IT support contact:

it-support@company.com

3. Manager Introduction

Employees should schedule an introductory meeting with their reporting manager during the first two business days.

Topics discussed typically include:

- Team structure
- Current projects
- Sprint process
- Expectations and KPIs
- Communication standards
- Performance goals

4. Team Collaboration Tools

Employees are expected to regularly use:

- Slack
- Jira
- Confluence
- Microsoft Teams
- GitHub
- Google Workspace

Training documentation for these tools is available through the internal knowledge base.

5. Mandatory Training

The following training modules must be completed within 14 days:

- Cybersecurity Awareness
- Workplace Harassment Prevention
- Accessibility Standards
- Data Privacy Compliance
- Secure Coding Practices

6. Onboarding Buddy Program

Each employee is assigned an onboarding buddy to assist with:

- Team introductions
- Process clarification
- Tool guidance
- Workplace culture
- Internal workflows

7. First Month Expectations

During the first month, employees are expected to:

- Complete all onboarding tasks
- Attend training sessions
- Understand project workflows
- Begin contributing to assigned tasks
- Participate in team meetings

Managers conduct a 30-day onboarding review to ensure smooth integration.
